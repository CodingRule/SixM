# Code of Conduct

SixM is built by volunteers. We want working on it to be pleasant for everyone, whatever their background or level
of experience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful. Accept feedback on your own work gracefully.
- Help newcomers. Everyone's first pull request was once their first.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, origin, body, beliefs or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing or requesting pirated game content, keys or leaked material. This also puts the project at risk.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions after being asked to stop.

## Where this applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere else you are acting on behalf of the
project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience something that breaks these rules, contact the maintainers privately through the
maintainers' GitHub profiles, or use GitHub's built-in "Report content" option on the offending comment. Do not
start a public argument about it.

Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers who are party to a report do not take part in handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break these rules. Depending on
severity and history, responses range from a private note, to a warning, to a temporary or permanent ban from the
project. Decisions are made in good faith and aim to be proportionate.
